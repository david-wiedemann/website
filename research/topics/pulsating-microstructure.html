Overview

Fast oscillations of a microstructure can modify macroscopic transport. In particular, an oscillating geometry may induce an effective advective contribution in a diffusion equation.

Pulsating microstructure at three scales

A travelling bending wave deforms the obstacle in each periodic cell. The fast temporal period is exactly the spatial period: \(T_\varepsilon=\varepsilon\).
