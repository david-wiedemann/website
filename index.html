Research

Research interests

My work lies at the interface of rigorous analysis, mathematical modelling, and multiscale methods.

Maxwell equations & wave propagation

Effective interface conditions for electromagnetic waves, thin periodic structures, polarization effects, and metasurface-type geometries.

→

Homogenization & multiscale media

Rigorous upscaling of PDEs in periodic and evolving microstructures, including porous media, transport processes, and fluid flow.

→

Calculus of variations

Polyconvexity, isotropic energy densities, nonlinear elasticity, and analytical and computational characterizations of constitutive models.
