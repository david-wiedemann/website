Optional background images for publications.html

Put any or all of these files in the same folder as publications.html:

background-publications.jpg   = All publications
background-maxwell.jpg        = Maxwell & wave propagation
background-homogenization.jpg = Homogenization & multiscale media
background-evolving.jpg       = Evolving microstructure
background-variations.jpg     = Calculus of variations
background-neural.jpg         = Neural networks

If one of these files is missing, background.jpg remains visible as the fallback.
Recommended: landscape images, ideally at least 1600 px wide.
